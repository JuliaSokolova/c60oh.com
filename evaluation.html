CHARACTERIZATION

Investigated at the University of Washington Molecular Analysis Facility.

C60OH Hydrogel has been examined using complementary analytical methods to document morphology, vibrational and functional-group features, and surface elemental / chemical-state information.

Raman

Representative original-matrix spectrum, 785 nm. Used to assess carbon-related vibrational and structural features.

ATR-FTIR

Representative original-matrix infrared spectrum used to assess hydroxyl-, water-, and oxygen-associated features.

XPS

Representative survey spectrum from UW MAF analysis, used to investigate detected surface elements and chemical-state information.

These methods provide complementary evidence about the material. No individual method is presented as standalone proof of fullerene molecular identity, purity, biological safety, or application performance.
